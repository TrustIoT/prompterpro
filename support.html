Support

Which iPhones work?

An iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence turned on (iPhone Settings, Apple Intelligence and Siri).

How do I get whispers without looking at my phone?

Set up Back Tap: in Prompter Pro, tap the hand icon on the home screen and follow the guide. Double tap gives a question, triple tap gives a comment.

I can't hear the whispers.

Connect your earbuds or glasses before you start listening, and check the volume. You can change the voice and speed in Settings, Voice.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Prompter Pro Premium, and tap Cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

I changed iPhone. How do I get Premium back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Prompter Pro, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases.

Is my conversation recorded?

No. Audio is never saved. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone, and the text is deleted when you stop listening. See our privacy policy.
