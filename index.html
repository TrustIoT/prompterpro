The right words, whispered in your ear.

Prompter Pro listens to your in-person conversations and, when you tap, whispers a smart question, a thoughtful comment or a witty line through your earbuds or smart glasses.

Get help

Whispers on tap

Double tap the back of your iPhone for a question, triple tap for a comment. Nobody sees you look at your phone.

Summaries

When you stop, Prompter Pro writes the key points and follow-ups, and can add them to Reminders.

Practice mode

Rehearse job interviews, networking and difficult conversations with a voice partner.

Private by design

Powered by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. No accounts, no tracking, and audio is never saved.

Requires an iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence turned on.
