Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Prompter Pro does not collect, store or sell your personal information. Your conversations stay on your iPhone.

Prompter Pro is an iPhone app made by Jibran Saleem, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Audio

Prompter Pro uses your iPhone's microphone only after you start listening or start a practice session. Apple's speech recognition turns speech into text on your iPhone. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent to us.

Transcripts

The text of a conversation is kept in your iPhone's memory while you listen and is deleted when you stop. It is never sent to us.

Suggestions and summaries

Apple Intelligence writes suggestions and summaries from parts of the transcript, on your iPhone. Where available, Apple's Private Cloud Compute may be used instead; Apple states that it does not store your data. We never receive it.

Voices

Prompter Pro's built-in voices run on your iPhone. The words they speak are never sent anywhere.

Your profile and settings

Your name, role, goals, personal dictionary and other settings are stored only on your iPhone. If you use "Recognise my voice", Prompter Pro saves one number describing how loud your voice is, not a recording.

Summaries and Reminders

Conversation summaries are stored only on your iPhone until you delete them. If you tap "Add follow-ups to Reminders", Prompter Pro asks your permission and only adds items; it does not read your other reminders.

Purchases

Apple handles subscriptions. We never see your payment details.

Analytics and tracking

Prompter Pro has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and no accounts.

Other people

Prompter Pro processes speech from the people you talk to. Please tell them when you use it, especially at work, and follow the law where you are.

Children

Prompter Pro is not intended for children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything Prompter Pro stores by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Email jibran.saleem@outlook.com with any question about your privacy.
